# SaaS Finance & Revenue Analytics

## Project Overview

This project analyzes a fictional SaaS business using MySQL.

The goal is to understand customer revenue, invoice performance, collections, outstanding receivables and AR aging.

## Business Questions

- How are customers distributed across segments?
- Which customer segments generate the most invoiced revenue?
- What is the average invoice value by segment?
- How many invoices are Paid, Open or Partially Paid?
- How much has been invoiced versus collected?
- Which customer segments have the highest outstanding receivables?
- How is outstanding AR distributed across aging buckets?
- Who are the highest-value customers?
- How does invoiced revenue change month by month?
- How does monthly revenue compare with the previous month?
- How do customers rank by total invoice value?
